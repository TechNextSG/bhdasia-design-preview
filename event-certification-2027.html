About TRE™

Become a Certified TRE™ Provider

TRE™ is a body-led series of simple, powerful exercises to release stress, tension and trauma and build resilience. It safely activates the body's natural reflex of shaking or vibrating, which helps calm the nervous system and release muscular tension — allowing the body to naturally return to a balanced state.

The Global TRE™ Provider Certification program allows certificate holders to offer TRE™ to the general public in both individual and group settings. The 2027 cohort is boosted by Internal Alchemy — Modules 2 and 3 each include an introductory Internal Alchemy workshop.

Only one cohort per year in Singapore — dates for 2027 are confirmed below.

The Certification Path

The 2027 Modules

Three modules — each a 2-day workshop plus online sessions — take you from personal practice to Certified Global TRE™ Provider.

1
Module 1 — Your Personal TRE™ Practice  ·  27 & 28 February 2027 · Singapore

For your personal use — can be purchased alone. Includes the 2-day workshop and 4 online TRE™ sessions: 2 personal one-to-one sessions led by a different TRE™ trainer, and 2 sessions experienced in a group setting.

2
Module 2 — Teaching TRE™ to One Person  ·  3 & 4 July 2027 · Singapore

Open after completing Module 1. Includes the 2-day workshop and 5 supervisions. 4 supervisions are individual and personal, facilitated by a different trainer; 1 supervision is in a group.

+
From Shaking to Shaping  ·  4 July 2027 · Singapore

A one-day From Shaking to Shaping session in Singapore, following Module 2.

+
Intro to Internal Alchemy — Lead One  ·  28 October 2027 · Singapore

A one-day introductory Internal Alchemy workshop focused on leading one-to-one work.

3
Module 3 — Teaching TRE™ to Groups  ·  30 & 31 October 2027 · Singapore

Open after completing Module 2. Includes the 2-day TRE™ workshop and 5 supervisions. 4 supervisions are individual and personal, facilitated by a different trainer; 1 supervision is in a group.

+
Intro to Internal Alchemy — Working with Groups  ·  31 October 2027 · Singapore

A one-day introductory Internal Alchemy workshop focused on working with groups.

✓
Global TRE™ Certified Provider

On completing all three modules you receive your Global TRE™ Provider certificate — qualified to offer TRE™ to the public in individual and group settings.
