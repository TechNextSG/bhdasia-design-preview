About the Workshop

What Is Internal Alchemy?

Internal Alchemy is an integrated system combining breathwork, fascial release, grounding and tremor work — restoring the nervous system at the level where tension actually lives.

This work brings more than twenty-five years of hands-on practice in bodywork, nervous system regulation and energetic healing into one integrated system, developed in close collaboration with Dr. David Berceli, founder of TRE™.

This is the first time Internal Alchemy is being offered in Singapore — a 2-hour evening introduction hosted at Nilayam Ashtanga Studio, brought to you by BHD Asia and HummingBeing.

In this session you will experience:

  • Breathwork to regulate and energise
  • Fascial release to unwind stored tension
  • Grounding practices for nervous system safety
  • Tremor work — the body's natural release reflex
