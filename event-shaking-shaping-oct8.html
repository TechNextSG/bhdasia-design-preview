About This Session

TRE™ in a Coaching Context

From Shaking to Shaping is a live online session exploring the use of TRE™ — Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises — in a coaching context. TRE™ activates the body's natural neurogenic tremoring response, releasing stored stress and tension so the nervous system can return to a balanced state.

Co-led by two certified TRE™ practitioners, the session looks at how coaches can bring body-based awareness into their client work — from embodied self-care and presence to recognising when and how somatic tools support a coaching conversation.

The session is a collaboration between Neurogenic Integration, BHD Asia and HummingBeing. Registration is handled by Neurogenic Integration.

Who It's For

This Session Is For

  • Coaches curious about somatic, body-based approaches
  • Practitioners exploring TRE™ before committing to certification
  • Therapists, counsellors and HR professionals working with stress and resilience
  • Anyone who wants to experience TRE™ live from home
