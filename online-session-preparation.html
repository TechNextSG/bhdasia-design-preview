Preparing for Your Online TRE™ Session

Everything to set up before we meet online — your space, your camera, your kit and your safety. Please read this through before your first session.

Before We Begin

Your Setup Is Part of the Practice

In an online session, everything your facilitator can offer you comes through the camera — watching how your body moves, how your tremors travel, and when it is time to pause. A few minutes of preparation lets us give you the same quality of attention you would receive in the room.

Please make sure you have enough space to place your device far enough away that we can see your whole body — standing upright during the preparation exercises, and lying on your mat during the TRE™ practice itself.

!

The one thing that matters most: we must be able to see you head to toe, both standing and on the mat. If we can only see part of you, we cannot guide you safely — so please test your camera before the session starts.

Camera Positioning

Where to Place Your Camera

Two views to check before every session — standing, and on the mat.

1. Standing — the preparation exercises

Step back until your feet and the top of your head are both inside the frame with a little room to spare. Landscape orientation, camera at roughly hip height.

2. On the mat — the tremoring practice

Set the device to the side of your mat so the full length of your body stays in shot, including your knees in the butterfly position.

  • Use landscape orientation and prop the device so it cannot slide or fall.
  • A larger screen helps — you need to follow the exercises being demonstrated.
  • Join a few minutes early and check your own video preview before we start.
  • Leave room to step forward and back without walking out of frame.
Your Checklist

What to Prepare

Have all of this ready and within reach before the session begins.

A yoga mat

Somewhere comfortable and non-slip to lie down for the tremoring practice.

Required

A device with a camera

Laptop, tablet or phone — the bigger the screen the better, so you can clearly see the exercises being shown.

Required

Wireless headphones

Essential in group classes, as you will be far from the device. In private sessions we can try without — but please have a pair ready to connect.

Required in group classes

Reliable internet

A stable connection throughout. If you can, sit close to your router or use a wired connection.

Required

Pillows, cushions, blankets

For support under your knees or head, and to stay warm as your body settles afterwards.

As needed

Good lighting

Light the room well and face the light where possible. If you are backlit or in shadow, we cannot see your movement.

Required

Loose, light-coloured trousers

Stretchy and comfortable enough for the butterfly position. Dark clothing hides your tremors from the camera, so please avoid black.

Required

Water within reach

Keep a glass or bottle beside your mat so you do not have to leave the frame.

Recommended

An undisturbed space

Let the household know, silence notifications, and close the door so you can let go completely.

Recommended
Safety First

Someone You Trust, Nearby

Please ask someone you trust to be in the same house or apartment as you — or right next door — for the duration of the session, and give us their name and phone number on your consent form.

This is rarely needed. It simply means that if you would benefit from someone physically present, we can reach them straight away. Having their details on the form is the important part.

The Platform

Joining on Zoom

Our online sessions and group classes run on Zoom. Only authenticated users can join, and only through the Zoom application — so please set this up in advance rather than on the day.

  1. Download the Zoom client app for your desktop or mobile device from zoom.us/download.
  2. Create or sign in to a Zoom account using the same email address you registered with.
  3. Test your camera and microphone inside the app before the session day.
  4. Join through the app, not the browser — joining from a browser or while signed out may block you from the session.
  5. If Zoom is new to you, allow ten extra minutes the first time. There are plenty of short walkthroughs online, and we are happy to help you test in advance.
On the Day

Fifteen Minutes Before

  1. Roll out your mat and clear the space around it.
  2. Set your device in place and check the two camera views above — standing, and lying down.
  3. Turn on the lights; close curtains if you are backlit.
  4. Change into loose, light-coloured trousers.
  5. Put cushions, a blanket and water beside your mat.
  6. Charge or plug in your device, and connect your headphones.
  7. Confirm your support person knows the session is happening.
  8. Open Zoom, sign in, and join a few minutes early.
