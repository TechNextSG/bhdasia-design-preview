Affiliations & Accreditations

The professional bodies, networks, and sister brands that Isabelle and BHD Asia are part of.

ICF — International Coaching Federation

Certified Member

The ICF is the world's largest organisation of professionally trained coaches. Isabelle holds ICF certification — the global gold standard for coaching credentials, ensuring rigorous ethical and competency standards.

About ICF

GloCoach

Executive Coaching Partner

Founded in 2018, GloCoach is a global leadership development platform delivering one-on-one executive and advisory coaching to business leaders in 30+ countries, with offices across Europe and Asia including Singapore. Isabelle partners with GloCoach as an executive coach, supporting leaders across its multinational client base.

Visit GloCoach

The Coaching Solution

UK Executive Coaching Partner

The Coaching Solution is a UK-based executive coaching consultancy providing bespoke coaching, team coaching and mentoring for senior leaders and C-suite executives — recognised among the Top 5 Executive Coaching Companies in the UK. Isabelle works with The Coaching Solution as one of its handpicked executive coaches.

Visit Website

TRE™ France

Francophone TRE™ Network

As a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer who coaches in French, Isabelle works with TRE™ France — the francophone network of certified TRE™ providers — supporting the growth of Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises across French-speaking communities.

About TRE™ Global

TRE™ Iberoamérica

Spanish-Speaking TRE™ Network

Isabelle collaborates with TRE™ Iberoamérica, the network connecting certified TRE™ providers across Spain, Portugal and Latin America — extending body-based stress release and resilience work to Spanish-speaking communities worldwide.

About TRE™ Global

Living with the Spirit

Sister Practice — Internal Alchemy

Founded by Sara and Simba Stenqvist and based in Chiang Mai, Thailand, Living with the Spirit is the home of Internal Alchemy — a somatic modality integrating breath, fascia and nervous-system regulation, developed in close collaboration with Dr. David Berceli, founder of TRE™. BHD Asia team member Simba brings this work to Singapore through our workshops and certification cohorts.

Visit Website

Languages à Gogo

Sister Brand — Language Training

Co-founded by Rouviere Teixeira, Languages à Gogo offers personalised language instruction with a unique human-centred approach: centring teaching around WHO you are, WHY you want to learn, and WHAT you need to achieve your goals.

Visit Website

Brainz Magazine

TRE™ for All

About TRE™
